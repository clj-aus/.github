## Why this exists

Australia doesn't currently have an active Clojure user group in any city.
Sydney had one — it went quiet when nobody kept it running. clj-aus is the
restart, built national from the start: any single Australian city is thin
on its own, but the country together is not.

We meet monthly. In person in Sydney, streamed for everyone else, and every
session recorded and published so the archive is worth something even if you
couldn't make the night.

## What we cover

Clojure and ClojureScript first, plus the wider family around them:
ClojureDart for mobile, Babashka for scripting, and newer hosts like jank
and Jolt. Talks run from production experience reports to language and
tooling deep-dives to beginner-friendly intros. Some months we skip the
talks and write code together instead.

## Speaking

Every session from #2 onward has a lightning-talk slot, and first-time
speakers get priority. Open an issue on
[`meetings`](https://github.com/clj-aus/meetings) or say so in the Meetup
comments.
